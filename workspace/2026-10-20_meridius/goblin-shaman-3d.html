Goblin Shaman 3D rig

The goblin shaman, built in Tripo with his staff and mask as separate models and rigged in Blender on a Mixamo-compatible skeleton. He comes in two variants, one in each mask: the horned skull, Tripo's mask trimmed to a face plate, and the bird skull with a feather crest on a red band, built from code in Blender. Both hold the staff closed in the right fist and carry the same tribal markings in dried blood. The goblin's idle, walk and death are retargeted onto both. As in his concept art, he crouches with his knees out and his back hunched, carries the staff upright in front of his right side with its foot off the ground, and holds his left hand out in front of his hip. The viewer below shows each shaman's clips, his standard pose and a head check, from the game's battle camera, in all four facings. There is no attack yet: he will mostly cast spells.

1 · Reference images

Nano Banana Pro on Higgsfield redrew the shaman as a T-pose and drew his staff and the horned skull mask on their own. The bird skull mask was drawn later, straight from the front, as the reference for building it in Blender.

2 · Tripo

Body, staff and horned mask as three models, Smart Mesh in quads, each textured at 4096 px. The body has 6,123 faces. 360 Tripo credits in all.

3 · Fixed in Blender

He stands 1.486 m tall, like the other goblins. The staff's leather grip is thinned from 5.7 to 3.6 cm, so his three-fingered fist closes round it.

4 · Two masks

The horned skull is cut behind the horns to a 0.34 m face plate and bent round his face. The beak mask is built from code: 4,575 faces, its eye sockets cut through in dark grey so his eyes show.

5 · Body paint

Tribal markings in dried blood, painted on his skin by script before the mask is fitted, so both shamans share one texture.

6 · Rigged

75 bones with Mixamo names, 52 of them deforming, at most four per vertex: fingers, forearm twist bones and skirt flaps. The staff rides in the right fist and the mask is rigid on the head.

7 · Checks

Turned, nodded or tilted, no body triangle passes through either mask; the face stays 1.3–1.4 mm off it. Only the fist's fingers touch the staff.

8 · Clips

The goblin's idle, walk and death, retargeted onto both rigs in a scratch copy, then crouched, hunched and given his concept's left arm. His right arm carries the staff in every frame, and the staff stays clear of his body and mask throughout.

Notes

  • Clips. The goblin's Mixamo idle (201 frames, 6.7 s), walk (36 frames, 1.2 s) and death (72 frames, 2.4 s) from projects/meridius/Animations, retargeted as the rabble's, with the hips' motion scaled to his height (0.65). The idle and the walk loop without a seam. The walk is in place, its 1.0 m/s kept as the clip's root speed. There is no attack: he will mostly cast spells, and the cast clips come later.
  • His concept's stance. Compared with his concept art, the clips stood too tall and too upright, with the left arm hanging. Now his hips sink 6 cm with his feet kept where they are, and his knees bend deeper and turn 15° outward: 55° and 64° in the idle instead of 24° and 36°. His back hunches 18° further forward in the idle, whose clip leaned 3° back, and 5° in the walk, whose clip already leaned 6° forward, so it now leans 9° to 13° forward in both. His head turns most of the hunch back, so he still looks ahead. His left hand is held out in front of his hip, where the concept holds a bone fetish, the elbow bent 71°. The idle keeps all its own left-arm motion, and the walk a third of its swing.
  • The staff in the idle and the walk. The clips were made for empty hands. His right arm keeps the standard pose on the clip's chest, so he holds the staff upright in front of his right side with his fist at chest height, as his concept art does. The chest's lean would tip the staff up to 22° in the idle and 23° in the walk, so the wrist takes back four fifths of it: it stays within 6° of upright in the idle and 7° in the walk. The crouch lowers his chest, so the fist rises by up to 14 cm in the idle and 18 cm where the walk's right footfall dips him, the elbow bending, to keep the staff's foot at least 12 cm off the ground. The staff stays at least 20 mm off his body and 10 mm off the skull mask. The beak mask's feathers would meet it in the walk, so that shaman's arm turns 7° out at the shoulder: the staff keeps 10 mm off the feathers, and its foot comes down to 9 cm off the ground in the idle. No body triangle passes through it in any frame.
  • The death. It starts from the idle's first pose, crouched, with the fist raised. As his back straightens out of the hunch, his arm and wrist keep the staff's turn and his head keeps its own a few frames longer, so the staff does not tip into his face. He lets go of the staff at frame 4, his fist opening as his arm goes over to the clip's. The staff topples about its foot, 45° to his right of ahead, and lies on the ground from frame 18. It falls clear of his body and at least 22 mm off his mask in every frame. The hips are raised or lowered so his lowest point lies on the ground in every frame, the open hand included, with no float and nothing below the ground.
  • Frames. Rendered in Blender's Workbench, flat-lit with an outline and cavity, from an orthographic camera 30° above the ground, at 254.5 px per metre (the rabble's scale), then packed at 0.75 scale. The clips, poses and head check are rendered in a 640 × 640 frame, the death in 1220 × 1000 at the same scale, so the fallen staff fits. Every frame stays at least 15 px inside its frame. The idle, walk and death sheets are made from Mixamo clips and stay out of Git; the shaman's README (projects/meridius/references/characters/blender/goblin_shaman/README.md) has the commands that render them.
  • Limits. The thumb lies along the staff's grip instead of folding over the index finger, as on the other goblins. Ten finger triangles cut the round grip, hidden inside the fist. The grip is at his hip height, so a fist that holds the staff upright cannot plant its foot on the ground: in the standard pose it stands 23 cm off it. No strap holds either mask on; the rig keeps it rigid on the head. The skirt passes through the legs a little in a stride, none in the standard pose. The clips are for review: they are not exported to Godot yet.

The viewer's frames are rendered from that copy by render_clips.py and packed by goblin_spear/tools/pack_sheets.py. The README there records the build and every check.
