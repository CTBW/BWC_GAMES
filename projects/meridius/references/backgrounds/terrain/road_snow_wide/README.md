# Wide snowy forest road

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_snow_wide__take-01.png](road_snow_wide__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable landscape concept.
  Continuous snow road with readable ruts, passing space and evergreen forest on both sides. Ivory snow and cool shaded edges; no troops or UI.
- [road_snow_wide_night__take-01.png](road_snow_wide_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The diagonal worn snow road, turning tracks, snowbanks, rocks, fallen logs and dense conifer edges match the daytime source. Warm sunlight is replaced by cool silver-blue light and deeper forest shade. Snow remains bright under moonlight, keeping wheel tracks and approaches readable; this is a bright moonlit view. No structures, lamps, fires or figures were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
