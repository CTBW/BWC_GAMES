# Small marching camp

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [camp_small_march__take-01.png](camp_small_march__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [camp_small_march__take-02.png](camp_small_march__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable camp concept.
  The approved tent groups, four gates, palisade, ditch, drill yard, well and supply awning match the original composition. Tall spruce, pine, birch and broadleaf layers now surround the clearing, with tangled ferns, mossy boulders, fallen wood and cool distant fog. The golden open parkland has become a more imposing northern forest while all gate approaches and camp structures stay readable. No troops, flames or UI.
- [camp_small_march_night__take-01.png](camp_small_march_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Cool moonlight and deeper woodland shadows retain the revised northern forest, complete palisade, gates, tent rows, stores and open drill yard. Paths and ditch crossings stay readable; no figures or new exterior fires. A few tent openings retain restrained warm colour against the cool canvas and ground.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
