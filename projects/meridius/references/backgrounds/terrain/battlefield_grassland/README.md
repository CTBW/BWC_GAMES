# Open grassland battlefield

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [battlefield_grassland__take-01.png](battlefield_grassland__take-01.png) — **Selected**; Source reviewed.
  Open cart tracks and broad rolling terrain, sparse edge trees; empty of units and UI.
- [battlefield_grassland_night__take-01.png](battlefield_grassland_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The shallow hills, eroded bank, intersecting worn tracks, scattered rocks and two tree clusters retain their source positions. Cool moonlight replaces the golden daytime lighting and the foreground shadows deepen without losing the routes. Natural vegetation colors remain distinguishable. No figures, buildings or artificial lights were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
