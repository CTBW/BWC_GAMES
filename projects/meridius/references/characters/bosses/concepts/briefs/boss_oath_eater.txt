MERIDIUS BOSS CONCEPT — THE OATH-EATER. Original design from this written brief; no image references.

CREATURE: A towering wolf-man with disproportionately long forearms, a narrow waist and a heavy upright mane. Charcoal fur, an old cream-white throat patch, amber eyes and a torn blue military sash. Broken eagle shields hang from a few iron rings at its waist; two immense scarred hands alternate between knuckle-running and grasping.

COMPOSITION: Show a full gigantic wolf-man at the end of a low charging skid in an original windswept oath court. Emphasise the long grasping forearms, narrow waist, heavy mane, old blue sash and a few broken eagle shields. One small braced squad receives his momentum, its ward offset behind, with reserves escaping laterally. Other company squads shelter near three massive oathstones. Keep all limbs readable and avoid an ordinary enlarged quadrupedal wolf, a furry human in armour, or a crowd of summoned wolves.

SCALE: About 9 metres upright. Exactly six tiny blue-and-cream human squads provide scale; they are separated formations, not six giant individuals. One boss only, no other creatures. Leave broad readable movement routes.

STYLE: Detailed hand-painted fantasy tactics illustration. Broad crisp brush shapes, textured natural materials, muted warm highlights and cool blue-green shadows. Elevated three-quarter strategy view with near-parallel perspective. Coherent upper-left light. Avoid glossy 3D, photorealism, heavy comic outlines and pixel filters.

OUTPUT: One 16:9 landscape composition. Keep the whole essential silhouette in frame with generous margin; only the Crown’s buried body may remain underground. No text, labels, HUD, grid, border, watermark, collage or reference insets. This is concept artwork, not a final game asset.
