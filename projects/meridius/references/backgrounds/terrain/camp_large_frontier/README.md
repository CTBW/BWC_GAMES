# Large frontier castra

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [camp_large_frontier__take-01.png](camp_large_frontier__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable camp concept.
  A complete large palisade and ditch encloses two extensive tent quarters, command pavilions, a broad drill field, supply and stable courts, a well and connected roads. Four timber gate structures and bridges remain fully in frame. Dense northern forest, mossy boulders, fallen wood and low fog surround a practical cleared apron with chopped stumps. Distinct from the river and small camps; no troops, active fires or UI.
- [camp_large_frontier_night__take-01.png](camp_large_frontier_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Compared directly with the daytime original: tent rows, command pavilions, immense drill ground, supply and stable courts, well, four bridged gates and chopped stumps retain their positions. Cool moonlight and low blue woodland fog replace the warm day lighting. The whole palisade, ditch and clear approach roads stay visible; existing tent interiors provide small warm accents, with no troops or added exterior flames.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
