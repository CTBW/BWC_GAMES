Meridius · 3D unit sandbox

Every 3D unit on one battlefield

Six rigged characters and a squad of six footmen, each in his own helmet and face, on a training ground in the game's own look: painted hills, the woodland, a pond, the fog of war and the night. March them over the map, set them on each other, drill the squad and play any of their clips.

Opens in its own maximized window. If the sandbox is already open, the button brings it to the front.

  1. Double-click Meridius Sandbox.cmd in the repository's root folder, E:\ProjektM\BWC_GAMES-godot, next to Setup.ps1. It does exactly what the button does.
  2. Or open prototypes/sandbox3d/scenes/sandbox3d.tscn in the Godot editor and press F6.
  3. To enable the button, update Mr. Mak once: open PowerShell from Start (outside Mr. Mak) and run 
    cd E:\ProjektM\BWC_GAMES-godot
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Setup.ps1 -Mode Update
    When it asks, quit Mr. Mak from the tray; it installs and starts again.

01Things to try

Everything happens with the mouse and a few keys. The left panel lists the roster and the selected unit's attacks, stances and clips; the right panel holds the switches.

Drill the footman squad

Click one of the six footmen in two ranks (or Footman squad in the left panel), then a dummy of the drill line: the squad advances in step, only the front rank strikes, each man in his own time, and the ranks re-form. Click the ground to march; Q / E wheel it.

March over the hills

Left-click a unit, then click the ground. It walks round trees, rocks and water and never up a bank too steep. Shift adds units, F follows them.

Strike a dummy or a foe

With a unit selected, click a foe or a straw dummy: it walks into reach and strikes. 1–8 pick the attack, Space strikes again.

Start a duel

Set the footman on the wolf scout. With Fight back on, a struck unit steadies and answers once the attacker is in its reach.

Lethal blows

Switch on Lethal blows and a landed blow kills, with one of the unit's death clips. K kills the selected unit, R revives it.

Fog of war and night

V turns on the fog, N the night. Trees fade while a unit stands behind them, as on the battlefield.

Play every clip

The left panel lists every clip in the selected model. Slow it all to 0.25x to read a motion, or run it at 2x. H plays the hit.

The squad

Click any of its men to select all six. Its panel: attack (1 / Space), re-form, wheel left or right, about face, halt, front rank only or spread to the line, hit, death, revive, stop, and each man alone.

Right panel

Fog (and forget the explored ground), grid, night, follow, outlines, shadows, the footman's 2D sprite beside him, lethal blows, fight back, the look (toon, flat, PBR), time scale 0.25x to 2x, and the camera at 1x, 2x, 3x or the whole field.

05Under the hood

For when something needs a closer look.

How the button works

  • This card lists one launcher in workspace/workspace.json: Meridius Sandbox.cmd in the repository root. The button asks Mr. Mak to run it; a card can start only the scripts its own entry lists.
  • The script runs projects/meridius/source/godot/tools/sandbox.ps1, which finds Godot 4.7.2 (or GODOT), opens the sandbox maximized, or brings an open one to the front.
  • Errors (no Godot, a missing scene) come back to the line under the button.

From a terminal

In projects/meridius/source/godot:

tools/godot.sh --path . res://prototypes/sandbox3d/scenes/sandbox3d.tscn

The pictures on this page: append -- --clean --capture=<folder>. A recorded tour: -- --tour with Godot's movie maker (--write-movie).

Checks

  • tools/test.sh test_sandbox3d: the map, routes, fog, the attacks' timing events, every roster model with its clips, and the squad's ranks and fights (only the front rank strikes, the blows staggered, a rear man closing up, the ranks re-formed).
  • The squad's pictures, with who struck when: append -- --capture=<folder> --shots=squad.
  • Frame cost with each part switched off in turn: prototypes/sandbox3d/qa/perf.gd.

Open points

  • The spearman's turn_90 clip turns only 67.5 degrees; the sandbox turns units by steering instead.
  • The crossbowman's walk speed comes from local Mixamo sources.
  • The wolf scout has no hit clip yet.
