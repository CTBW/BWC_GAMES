# Black root-rot marsh

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [corruption_root_rot__take-01.png](corruption_root_rot__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable corruption concept.
  Rotten dark stumps and exposed roots dominate the marsh, with pale shelf fungus, dead standing trees, stagnant green pools and dirty surface films. A raised dry route crosses the connected damp channels. Surviving woodland at the outer edge contrasts with the dead interior, with low fog adding depth. Distinct from the red-fungus landscape and free of troops or UI.
- [corruption_root_rot_night__take-01.png](corruption_root_rot_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The night treatment preserves the rotten stumps, exposed roots, connected stagnant pools, dry road and fog placement of the daytime marsh. Cool moonlight catches pale shelf fungi and dying grass while deep shadows retain bark and ground detail. Green-brown water and the surviving outer woodland keep their material colours; the crossing remains legible. No added figures, torches or magical glow.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
