# Thick northern forest — clear daylight

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_forest_northern_clear__take-01.png](road_forest_northern_clear__take-01.png) — **Selected**; forest comparison.
  Taller spruce/pine, birch trunks, ferns, mossy rocks and fallen timber create a stronger northern woodland character. Worn road remains clear and continuous. Daylight is warmer than the neutral-light prompt, with slight distant atmospheric haze; the central clearing is still broad. Preserve as the clear-day comparison while the darker, foggier version explores much denser woodland.
- [road_forest_northern_clear_night__take-01.png](road_forest_northern_clear_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The winding cart road, rocky right bank, tall spruce and pine, birches, mixed shrubs, fern beds, stumps and fallen wood retain the accepted daylight arrangement. Cool silver-green moonlight replaces golden sunlight, with deep blue-green woodland shadows. The road and sloping ground remain readable. This is a bright moonlit treatment, with a soft cool haze at the upper-left light source. No buildings, figures, torches or artificial lights were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
