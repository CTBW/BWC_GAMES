# Dark forest battlefield

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [battlefield_dark_forest__take-01.png](battlefield_dark_forest__take-01.png) — **Selected**; Source reviewed.
  Two linked clearings, damp ground and old trees. Camera is lower than other scene concepts; adjust during map design.
- [battlefield_dark_forest_night__take-01.png](battlefield_dark_forest_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The immense twisting trunks, central clearing, raised right bank, exposed roots, rocks and small streams retain their daytime positions. The warm opening through the left trees becomes cool moonlight, with deeper green-blue shade under the canopy. Both the lower clearing and upper approach stay legible. No added structures, figures, fires or lamps.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
