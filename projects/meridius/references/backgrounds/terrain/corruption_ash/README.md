# Ash-choked corrupted forest

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [corruption_ash__take-01.png](corruption_ash__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable corruption concept.
  Grey ash-covered soil, bleached dead trees, blackened fallen timber, pale fungi and cracked earth create a distinct dying landscape. A worn raised road crosses the dry drainage basin; the route remains readable. Healthy foliage is confined to the distant upper edge, with low fog and subdued light through the dead woodland. No troops, torches or UI.
- [corruption_ash_night__take-01.png](corruption_ash_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The dry basin, raised road, bleached trees, blackened timber and pale fungi retain the daytime layout. Cool night shadows and silver highlights deepen the ash-covered woodland while preserving readable ground and the narrow healthy forest boundary. The dry channels remain empty, with no introduced water, flames, figures or UI.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
