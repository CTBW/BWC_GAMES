# Working preferences

This is a starting point. Replace it with your preferences when you are ready.

- Stored content: English unless explicitly requested otherwise.
- Communication: clear, practical updates and visible results.
- Reports: shared Workspace styling, readable tabs, image enlargement and downloads.
- Changes: keep revisions together and record what was checked.
- Permissions: choose the level appropriate for each task; do not infer a personal
  preference for bypassing approvals from the examples.
- Restarts: ask before interrupting a running application or agent chat.
- Image generation (2026-10-24): use Higgsfield website Unlimited for image work.
  Verify Unlimited is ON (green) before every submission. Consult the other
  agent for the established workflow if needed. Do not switch to paid mode or
  another provider without the user's instruction.
- Creative pipeline downloads (2026-10-24): automatically download the images,
  models and textures produced by the requested Higgsfield / Tripo / Blender
  workflow into the relevant project, verify them and record their local paths.
  No separate download confirmation is needed. Paid-action budgets still apply.
- Tripo credits (2026-10-23): don't spend freely. Ask before each paid Tripo
  action (generate, texture, segment, rig) and state its cost; prefer free routes
  such as Blender for mesh edits. Generate one variant for the character and one
  for each related item (weapon, quiver, shield), then decide from there.
