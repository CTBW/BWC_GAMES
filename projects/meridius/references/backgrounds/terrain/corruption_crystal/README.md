# Violet crystal blight

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [corruption_crystal__take-01.png](corruption_crystal__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [corruption_crystal__take-02.png](corruption_crystal__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable corruption concept.
  The revised image preserves the winding road, rock ridges and violet crystal formations while replacing vigorous golden grass with grey-brown dead vegetation, pale mineral crusts and leached soil. Bleached trees and dusty purple foliage reinforce the blight; healthy woodland remains at the outer boundary. The road retains warm earth colour but the overall light is subdued and cool. No emissive neon, figures or UI.
- [corruption_crystal_night__take-01.png](corruption_crystal_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The corrected blight layout is preserved: winding road, crystal ridges, exposed roots, pale mineral crusts and dying trees stay in place. Cooler blue ambient shadows and moonlit highlights replace the subdued daytime lighting. Violet crystals retain a physical reflective appearance without neon emission; the pale ground and road remain readable. The change is restrained because the daytime source was already overcast. No added troops, torches or UI.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
