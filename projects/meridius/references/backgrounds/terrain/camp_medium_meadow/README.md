# Medium meadow castra

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [camp_medium_meadow__take-01.png](camp_medium_meadow__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [camp_medium_meadow__take-02.png](camp_medium_meadow__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable camp concept.
  The original tent blocks, command pavilion, four defended gates, drill ground, supply and stable yards, wells and road connections remain in place. Denser northern woodland now frames the outer edges with spruce, pine, birch, deadwood and undergrowth. Cooler subdued light replaces the strong golden glow; distant haze is subtle. The useful open meadow and defensive apron are preserved. No troops, flames or UI.
- [camp_medium_meadow_night__take-01.png](camp_medium_meadow_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The full palisade, four defended gates, tent blocks, supply and stable courts, wells and drill ground retain their daytime positions. Cool moonlit canvas, subdued meadow and deeper forest shadows clearly separate the night treatment; the command pavilion has a small warm interior glow. Roads, gate bridges and defensive clearances stay legible without exterior fires, troops or added scenery.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
