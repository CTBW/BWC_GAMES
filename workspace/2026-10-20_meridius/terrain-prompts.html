Art direction

Keep the elevated isometric camera, layered painted foliage, worn stone and timber, warm highlights and cool forest shade. Build hills from continuous slopes and eroded banks. Remove the references' units, grids and health bars. Use single isolated objects for extraction and full landscape compositions for battlefield concepts.
