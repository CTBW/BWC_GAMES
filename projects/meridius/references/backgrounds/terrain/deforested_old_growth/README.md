# Felled ancient forest and dry ravine

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [deforested_old_growth__take-01.png](deforested_old_growth__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable deforested landscape concept.
  Large ancient stumps with clear age rings and mossy roots dominate the felled clearing. Weathered fallen trunks and skeletal trees frame a completely dry diagonal ravine with rounded stones and curled mud. Worn paired wagon tracks bend around the largest stump, and sloped entries connect to the dry channel. The brushwork is more strongly outlined than the basin take, but the elevated framing and natural materials remain coherent. No people, machinery, water or UI.
- [deforested_old_growth_night__take-01.png](deforested_old_growth_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The exact huge ringed stumps, felled trunks, dead trees, curving cart track and dry diagonal ravine remain in place. Cool moonlit stump tops and stone, deeper blue-green shadows and subdued dead grass replace the daytime amber light. The sloped crossings and dry sediment remain legible. No buildings, artificial lights, water or figures were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
