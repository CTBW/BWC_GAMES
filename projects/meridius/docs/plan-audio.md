# Meridius: sound

Outcome: the battles sound real and close. Steel rings on steel, boots and
armour move with the men, a fireball roars in and bursts, trolls shake the
ground, wolves snarl and the collie guard growls. The camp breathes: wind in
the pines, northern birdsong, a crackling fire. Every sound is tied to the
real game event and the place it happens on screen.

## Decisions (2026-10-22)

- Recorded sound from free libraries wherever possible: Sonniss GDC Game Audio
  Bundles, Freesound (CC0 and CC-BY only), Kenney (CC0), Pixabay, OpenGameArt
  CC0. No paid packs. No BBC Sound Effects (non-commercial licence).
- Spoken commands, war cries and shouts: Higgsfield speech (Eleven v4 through
  the Higgsfield MCP, about 0.2 credits a line). Pitch and EQ edits for orcs
  and goblins are allowed; originals are kept.
- Music: Suno, generated by the owner on the Pro plan (commercial rights for
  songs made while subscribed; free-plan songs may not be used). WAV downloads.
- AI sound effects (fal) only for a gap no library fills, and only after asking.

The repository (`CTBW/BWC_GAMES`) is public on GitHub. The Sonniss licence
forbids redistributing its sounds, edited or not, so Sonniss-derived runtime
files must not be committed while it is public. CC0 files (Freesound CC0,
Kenney, OpenGameArt CC0) and our own Suno output may be. The Higgsfield
voices stay local too (the owner's choice, 25 Oct 2026): `build_bank.py`
renders them into the gitignored `assets/audio/bank/local/`.

Each take moves through: candidate → liked (for comparison) → accepted →
integrated → mix-reviewed. Rejected takes stay in the library, marked
rejected. Edits (trim, fade, gain, pitch) are recorded with the source file
and its hash; an accepted original is never replaced silently.

## Sound by event

The game already fires 42 named sounds from real rule events
(`scripts/render/battle_view.gd`, `scripts/app/app.gd`). The upgrade replaces
their content and adds the missing layers.

| Event | Layers | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee blow | effort grunt (optional) → swing air → contact → victim reaction | Contact only on a real hit: steel on steel (`block`), blade into mail/plate (`hit_metal`), blade into flesh (`hit_flesh`), whoosh only (`miss`) |
| Orc brute / troll blow | heavy swing → club or log smash → ground thump | `smash`; trolls hit like a falling tree |
| Steps | boot on dirt/grass + armour jingle; goblin light feet; troll stomp; wolf and dog paws | Per unit type, coalesced per squad, ideally on planted feet |
| Fireball | gather/charge → launch whoosh → flight roar → explosion + debris | `cast`, `fireball`, `explosion` |
| Lightning, frost, heal, hex, bloodlust | crack + thunder tail; ice crackle; warm shimmer; dark pulse | |
| Bow, crossbow, ballista, catapult, rock lobber | release + string; whiz; impact by material | `bow`, `crossbow`, `bolt_hit`, `ballista`, `catapult`, `crash` |
| War wagon | wooden rumble + creak → ram crash | |
| Deaths | body fall + gear clatter; voice separate | `death`, `death_player` |
| Horns and drums | human war horn, crude goblin horn, war drums | `horn`, `warhorn`, `drum` |
| UI | page, click, deny, coin, build | Flat, on the UI bus |

## Music (Suno, owner generates)

Same key and tempo for the battle tracks so they can crossfade by intensity.
Instrumental on, WAV download, two to four takes per prompt. Put the downloads
in `local/audio-library/music/suno/<track>/` (repository root).

1. **Camp, Amberfeld garrison:** Nordic folk, calm and warm, nyckelharpa
   melody, soft tagelharpa drone, wooden flute, gentle frame drum, light
   strings, cold morning forest, hopeful but weary, D dorian, 72 BPM,
   instrumental, steady, no big ending.
2. **Campaign map:** Nordic folk journey, Hardanger fiddle and cello, hand drum
   pulse, open fifths, wide and cold, a march into wild country, A minor,
   90 BPM, instrumental.
3. **Battle, tension:** dark Nordic war ambience, low tagelharpa drone, sparse
   deep frame drums, low string ostinato, distant horn calls, held breath
   before the fight, D minor, 100 BPM, instrumental, no melody climax.
4. **Battle, combat:** epic Nordic battle music, pounding war drums, driving
   low strings, wordless deep male choir, tagelharpa, brass stabs, relentless,
   D minor, 100 BPM, instrumental.
5. **Final wave, Orc Warlord:** brutal tribal war music, huge drums, throat
   singing drone, distorted low horns, war chant without words, menacing,
   D minor, 100 BPM, instrumental.
6. **Victory (15–20 s):** triumphant Nordic fanfare, horns and wordless choir,
   resolving to D major, ends cleanly.
7. **Defeat (15–20 s):** solemn lament, solo cello and low horn, fading,
   D minor.
8. **Main menu:** slow Nordic saga theme, nyckelharpa and choir, misty and
   grand, D minor, 80 BPM, instrumental.

Loop points, crossfades and the battle intensity switch are cut and wired in
the game; Suno's intros and endings do not need to be perfect. `build_bank.py`
cuts a loop to whole bars from the track's tempo (a `recipe.json` take with
`start`, `bpm` and `bars`), so the downloads need no editing.

## Phases

1. **Engine:** buses (Master, Music, Ambience, SFX, Voice, UI) with a limiter
   and gentle ducking; volume sliders saved in prefs; sound placed at the
   unit's screen position; take variation without repeats; voice limits;
   footsteps by unit type; ambience and music players; tests and docs.
2. **First five:** sword clash, fireball, camp forest, boots, troll stomp.
   Candidates → owner picks by ear → integrated → checked in-game at battle
   distance.
3. **Rollout:** every remaining event, creatures, voices, battle ambience,
   UI, music.
4. **Mix:** loudness balance, a full horde battle, pause, mute and scene
   changes, normal speakers and headphones.
