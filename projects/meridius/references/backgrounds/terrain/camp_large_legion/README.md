- [camp_large_legion_night__take-01.png](camp_large_legion_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.

Tall northern woodland and low fog surround the complete enclosure. Cool moonlit canvas, darker timber and forest shadows establish night; a few existing tent openings have modest warm interior light. All principal routes remain readable, with no troops or new exterior fires.
