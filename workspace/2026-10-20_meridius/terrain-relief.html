MERIDIUS / NATIVE GODOT TERRAIN STUDY / 24 OCTOBER 2026

Relief ground: hills, banks and slopes

Open Terrain Test Fields on the title screen and choose Bluff and hollow or Rolling meadow. This pass reworks the ground under the woodland: real raised ground with crumbling earth banks, and gentle hills that read through their light. Trees and the lab screen are unchanged.

Result: the bluff now stands as a plateau with a stratified face of sandy soil and pale stone, turf hanging over its crest and fallen blocks at its foot. Gentle slopes turn golden toward the sun and cool teal away from it, as in the hill study. All captures are native Godot (GL Compatibility, 1920×1080) with zero recorded errors. This is still a lab-only renderer and has not been accepted for battles. Newest: the painted woodland from the Woodland scouting trial now replaces the Blender trees and bushes everywhere in the game, battles included, and the painted stones and boulders now carry the terrain's own rock texture.

Painted woodland in battles

The painted pine, beech, oak and shrub from the Woodland scouting trial are now the game's trees and bushes. Battles, the campaign map and the garrison draw them in place of the low-poly Blender trees. Each painting is cut out of its magenta backdrop and keeps its own colour. The shadow the game draws for props, falling down and to the right like the painted light, is baked in. Trees keep the lab's sizes, so a pine is about a fifth taller than before. The shrub covers the old bush's footprint. Map layouts, sight lines and rules are unchanged; only the drawing changed.

Checks: native battle (deploy, march, night), campaign and garrison captures with zero errors. Frame times match the Blender trees in back-to-back runs (battle_fps.gd and the campaign capture). test_assets passes. Sources: tools/props/woodland_cutout.py, assets/sprites/painted/woodland_*.webp, the tree_pine, tree_oak and bush entries in assets/sprites.json; paintings in assets/terrain/woodland/.

Bigger fallen blocks and painted rocks

The fallen blocks at the bluff's foot are now twice as large, about three quarters of a tile across: chunky rounded slabs with a lit top and rim, a lit left face, a cool shaded right face and a little moss, often with a smaller stone beside them. They are part of the lab-only relief ground.

The Stones and Boulders props are repainted in concept A's style: four variants of each, cut from one Nano Banana Pro sheet (Higgsfield, 4 credits) with a soft shadow falling down and to the right. Unlike the relief ground they are live in the game: battles, the campaign map and the garrison draw them. Map layouts and rules are unchanged; only the drawing changed.

Textured with the terrain's rock

The stones now take their surface from the terrain folder's rock texture (assets/terrain/rock.webp). The texture's grass tufts and lichen are removed first, then its colour and fine grain go under the painted light, outline and moss. The props now read as the same grey stone as the bluff's strata and fallen blocks; the first pass stood out cream and pink. The images below the texture board show that first pass.

Raised ground with banks

The new field is a three-level plateau, steep toward the camera with a long ramp behind it, a track along its foot and a smaller rise to the east. Its face is painted upright in screen space: thick level beds of sandy soil and darker loam, now and then a pale stone slab, each bed with a lit ledge and a soft undercut, and only a few open cracks. The turf hangs over the crest in ragged tongues, and faceted blocks fallen from the face lie along its foot, lit like the rock props. Patrols can climb the ramp to the top.

The second pass (24 October, afternoon) removed the masonry look of the first: blocks four times longer, most joints closed, no stone checkerboard and no stepped brightness from block to block.

Gentle hills that read

The hills' light was already correct, but it was lost in a busy, uniformly green texture. The lit flank of the meadow's main hill now measures about 1.6× the flat ground and the shaded flank about 0.7× (mist off). The painted textures keep about 70% of their detail contrast, and the slopes shift hue, golden toward the sun and teal away from it.

Targets

Concept A is the look for raised ground; the hill study shows how a gentle slope should read through hue as much as value.

Open points

  • The lab's drifting mist lifts shaded slopes noticeably and flattens the gentle hills. Lowering it in open ground would help the slopes read.
  • On the bluff's curved west end the strata follow the height lines and still show as faint arcs. The meadow's grass strokes are drawn over the fallen blocks.
  • The painted woodland has one painting per tree type, shown at three sizes. A dense forest repeats the same pine; more paintings per type would break that up. The ferns from the lab are not in battles yet, since they need a change in the battle renderer.
  • In the terrain lab the Painted woodland toggle now compares the lab's keyed paintings with these sprites, not with the Blender trees.
  • Relief stays lab-only. Production battles keep the current renderer until the new terrain is accepted.
