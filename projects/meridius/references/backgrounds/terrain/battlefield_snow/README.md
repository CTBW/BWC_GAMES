# Snow battlefield

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [battlefield_snow__take-01.png](battlefield_snow__take-01.png) — **Selected**; Source reviewed.
  Connected snowy approaches with readable drifts, rock edges and pine borders.
- [battlefield_snow_night__take-01.png](battlefield_snow_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The intersecting snowy tracks, two rounded hills, exposed rock faces and enclosing winter trees retain the exact daytime layout. Cool silver-blue snow replaces the warm sunlight, with deeper blue forest shadows and readable trail depressions. The scene remains empty and no buildings, fires, lights or figures were introduced.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
