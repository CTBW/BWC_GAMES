# Blood-fungus corrupted valley

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [corruption_blood_fungus__take-01.png](corruption_blood_fungus__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [corruption_blood_fungus__take-02.png](corruption_blood_fungus__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable corruption concept.
  Revised to the user's grey, sick and dying direction. Ashen soil, collapsed dry vegetation, pallid trunks and bare branches replace the first take's healthy golden landscape. Red fungus and pale mycelium remain distinct against the muted surroundings; grey-green water carries dark silt and fungal staining. Stream, ford, routes and major colonies retain the original composition. No troops or UI.
- [corruption_blood_fungus_night__take-01.png](corruption_blood_fungus_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The night view preserves the revised grey, dying landscape: stream, ford, roads, tree trunks and red fungal colonies match the daytime source. Cooler moonlit highlights and deeper shadows replace the diffuse daytime light while retaining murky water, pale mycelium and dark crimson fungus. Routes remain readable; no healthy green meadow, added torches, figures or neon growth.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
