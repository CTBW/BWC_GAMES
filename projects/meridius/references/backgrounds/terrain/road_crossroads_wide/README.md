# Natural forest crossroads with signpost

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_crossroads_wide__take-01.png](road_crossroads_wide__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable landscape concept.
  Four open natural approaches, wheel wear, drainage and gravel repairs, with the requested wooden direction sign safely on the shoulder. Sign is prominent; scale can be reduced during layout preparation.
- [road_crossroads_wide_night__take-01.png](road_crossroads_wide_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The four approaches, turning wheel ruts, compacted crossing, loose gravel, drainage channels, rocks and roadside wooden sign preserve the daytime layout. Cool silver-blue light replaces the warm sunlight, with deep green-blue forest shade. The junction stays empty and all four approaches remain legible. No figures, buildings, torches or lamps were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
