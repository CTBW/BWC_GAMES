Meridius · Unit roster

Height chart

Every 3D unit in the sandbox at the same scale, from the front and from the side, with its height, width and depth. Click a unit to try a new height or width. The chart, its card and the table update, and the change is written out for you to send on.

Each unit is in its idle at the first frame, drawn in Godot in the game's toon look and measured from the picture (2.5 mm a pixel). Height is the top of the head (helmet, hood, ears or horns) above the ground. Width and depth are the body's, without the weapons and shields it holds. Measured 26 October 2026.

At a glance
  • The tallest are the Wolf Scout (1.95 m to the ear tips), the Orc Brute (1.93 m) and the Succubus (1.91 m to the horns). The Goblin Rabble is the smallest at 1.24 m. The goblin archers and spearmen are 1.46 m.
  • The garrison's men run from the Battle Mage (1.70 m, stooped under his hood) and the Footman (1.74 m in his helmet) to the Spearman (1.80 m) and the Crossbowman (1.87 m).
  • The Footman no longer sinks into the ground. Every clip of his export stood 10.4 cm too low; it has been re-exported standing on the ground, in the sandbox as well.
  • The game's own height value (what places effects and markers) is off by more than 5 cm for the Spearman, the Battle Mage and the Wolf Scout 2. Each card says by how much.

How it is measured

    
    
      
  • The units are the sandbox's own (prototypes/sandbox3d/scripts/roster.gd), loaded as the sandbox loads them. The footman
        wears his squad's dress and helmet. Each is held on the first frame of his idle, the pose he stands in on the field.
    • 
      
  • An orthographic camera draws each one at 400 pixels a metre, from the front and from his right side. The opaque pixels give the
        extent: once with everything, once with the held weapons and shields hidden. The ink outline is left out of the measurement.
    • 
      
  • Squad spacing and size spread come from the squads' drills (squad3d.gd DRILLS). The game height is the
        sidecar's height_m.
    • 
      
  • Trying a size changes nothing in the game. Height scales the whole unit evenly (the profile's scale). Width is an
        extra widening on top of it, applied to width and depth together.
    • 
      
  • To measure again after a model changes, run this from projects/meridius/source/godot (it needs a window):
        
    tools/godot.sh --path . --audio-driver Dummy -s res://tests/visual/unit_heights.gd
