# Clear-cut basin and braided dry channels

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [deforested_river_basin__take-01.png](deforested_river_basin__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable deforested landscape concept.
  A broad entirely dry basin contains branching worn channels, gravel bars, rounded stones, cracked mud and bleached driftwood. Chopped stumps, cut trunks and a few skeletal dead trees occupy the eroded banks, with surviving woodland confined mostly to the perimeter. The connected channel floor provides broad movement space and distinct elevation edges. No water, people, machinery or UI.
- [deforested_river_basin_night__take-01.png](deforested_river_basin_night__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [deforested_river_basin_night__take-02.png](deforested_river_basin_night__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Strict lighting edit preserves the exact branching dry channels, gravel bars, cracked sediment, stumps, felled timber, eroded banks and surviving forest. Cool silver-blue light replaces golden sunlight while keeping ground detail readable. No added water or structures. Take 1 was rejected for three invented cabins at the upper-right forest edge; this take restores the empty woodland.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
