# Wide ancient-forest road

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_dark_forest_wide__take-02.png](road_dark_forest_wide__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable refined concept.
  The original woodland composition is preserved. A modest unlettered directional sign now stands on the right-hand shoulder outside the turning area. The roads have smoother broad turning arcs, paired worn wheel routes, irregular verges and small gravel repairs; roots were cleared from the busiest central approach while remaining along forest edges. No figures, extra buildings or UI. Use this revised source for the night view.
- [road_dark_forest_wide_night__take-01.png](road_dark_forest_wide_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The four worn approaches, mature trunks, fern beds, rocks and wooden sign retain the corrected daytime layout. The sign remains beside the crossing and the central road opening stays clear. Warm sunlight becomes pale cool moonlight with deeper green-blue woodland shade. No figures, structures, torches or lamps were added.
