# Imposing northern forest — dark and foggy

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_forest_northern_fog__take-01.png](road_forest_northern_fog__take-01.png) — **Selected**; forest comparison.
  Dense taller conifers, birch and old broadleaf trees, mossy rocks, tangled ferns and fallen timber enclose the worn road. Layered mist runs between trunks and through hollows without hiding the main route. More imposing and cooler than the clear-day comparison, although the foreground grass still retains warm golden highlights. No figures or UI.
- [road_forest_northern_fog_night__take-01.png](road_forest_northern_fog_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Faithful match to the daytime forest: the road curve, major trees, rocks, fallen logs and fog placement remain consistent. Cool moonlight replaces golden sunlight, with readable green foliage, grey timber, stone and the route through the forest. Shadows retain detail and no figures, torches or other new objects were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
