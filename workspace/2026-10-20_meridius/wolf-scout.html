MERIDIUS / CHARACTER RIGS / WOLF SCOUTS

Wolf scout vanguard

White fur, green eyes, olive cloth and brown leather. The supplied character design is the source for a modular scout: body, flowing hooded cloak, expressive tail and two matching daggers.

Current stage: Wolf Scout 3 is the only Wolf Scout in the 3D unit sandbox, a squad of three on the Amberfeld side, on the south flank of the player line. She rests in a crouch with both daggers held point back, her hooded cape draped over her back and shoulders, her ears through its hood. All four clips are in the pose viewer below.
