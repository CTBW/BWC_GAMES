- [battlefield_town_night__take-01.png](battlefield_town_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The central cobblestone junction, half-timber houses, well, empty market stalls and surrounding rocky banks retain their daytime layout. No people, added fixtures or new structures. This is a bright moonlit treatment rather than a deeply dark night.
