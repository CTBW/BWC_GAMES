MERIDIUS BOSS CONCEPT — RAUK, THE LAST BELL. Original design from this written brief; no image references.

CREATURE: A slab-bodied grey ogre beneath an asymmetrical timber-and-iron bell yoke. An enormous cracked bronze bell hangs beside its chest; one hand grips its rim and the other drags a gatehook. Scarred skin, blunt tusks, chain bites and faded red Host cloth. The bell is almost the size of a cottage.

COMPOSITION: Show Rauk at the instant before a lateral bell swing, the bell rim larger than a six-soldier shield squad. His full body, yoke, bell, hook and feet fit inside the frame. Place six small blue-and-cream company squads around a newly designed ruined ridge signal court, two bracing separately in front and reserves visibly offset. No literal coloured game overlays. The enormous bronze bell and grey organic body must dominate, with generous readable ground between combatants.

SCALE: About 12 metres tall. Exactly six tiny blue-and-cream human squads provide scale; they are separated formations, not six giant individuals. One boss only, no other creatures. Leave broad readable movement routes.

STYLE: Detailed hand-painted fantasy tactics illustration. Broad crisp brush shapes, textured natural materials, muted warm highlights and cool blue-green shadows. Elevated three-quarter strategy view with near-parallel perspective. Coherent upper-left light. Avoid glossy 3D, photorealism, heavy comic outlines and pixel filters.

OUTPUT: One 16:9 landscape composition. Keep the whole essential silhouette in frame with generous margin; only the Crown’s buried body may remain underground. No text, labels, HUD, grid, border, watermark, collage or reference insets. This is concept artwork, not a final game asset.
