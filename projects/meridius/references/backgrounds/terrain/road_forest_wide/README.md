# Wide mixed-forest road

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [road_forest_wide__take-01.png](road_forest_wide__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable landscape concept.
  Continuous rolling ground, worn curving cart road, forest on both sides and connected open approaches. Clear warm/cool painted materials. Broad overview, though trees remain larger than the requested distant scale.
- [road_forest_wide_night__take-01.png](road_forest_wide_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The winding diagonal road, upper fork, two rounded rocky hills and mixed woodland retain the daytime arrangement. Silver-green moonlit grass and cool road surfaces replace the warm sunlight, while the dense edge trees fall into blue-green shade. The main route and quiet clearing remain legible. No buildings, fires, lamps or figures were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
