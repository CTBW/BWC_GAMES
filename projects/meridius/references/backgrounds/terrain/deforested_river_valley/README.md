# Logged valley and dry river

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [deforested_river_valley__take-01.png](deforested_river_valley__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable landscape concept.
  Strong sweeping dry riverbed, eroded banks, exposed roots, chopped stumps, felled logs and standing dead trees. Open ford and logging approaches connect the terrain. Slight atmospheric recession at the far edge; no troops or UI.
- [deforested_river_valley_night__take-01.png](deforested_river_valley_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Direct comparison confirms the same winding dry channel, crossing track, chopped stumps, felled logs, dead trees and exposed bank roots. Cool moonlit stone and subdued grass replace the golden daytime palette, with deeper woodland shadows. The channel remains visibly dry and the route through it stays clear; no added water, figures, lights or structures.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
