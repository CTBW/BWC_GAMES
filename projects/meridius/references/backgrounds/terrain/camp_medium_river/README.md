# Medium camp above a riverbank

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [camp_medium_river__take-01.png](camp_medium_river__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable camp concept.
  The complete medium camp stands on a dry river terrace with four bridged gate openings, orderly tent blocks, central command pavilion, a broad drill yard, stores and a well. A gently descending approach connects to a separate timber river crossing outside the palisade. Tall northern woodland, deadwood and low mist frame the clearing without blocking routes. A tiny lit torch beside the rear stores is an unrequested detail to remove during asset preparation; no army figures or UI appear.
- [camp_medium_river_night__take-01.png](camp_medium_river_night__take-01.png) — Retained take; Earlier or unreviewed take; retained for comparison.
- [camp_medium_river_night__take-02.png](camp_medium_river_night__take-02.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  Strict lighting edit restores the approved medium camp composition: all tent blocks, four bridged gates, rear stores and well, central command tent, foreground drill yard and separate river bridge are preserved. Cool moonlight replaces the golden day palette while the river, roads and ditch stay clear. The tiny rear torch is extinguished; only a restrained command-tent interior glow remains. Take 1 was rejected for redesigning the camp into a small outpost.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
