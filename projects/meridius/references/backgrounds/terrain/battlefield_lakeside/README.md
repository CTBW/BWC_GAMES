# Lakeside battlefield

Original PNG copies. Selected means the take used in the terrain review gallery; read its limitations before using it as an asset.

- [battlefield_lakeside__take-01.png](battlefield_lakeside__take-01.png) — **Selected**; Source reviewed.
  Wide dry shoreline route and transparent shallows. Steeper eroded banks remain at the landward edge.
- [battlefield_lakeside_night__take-01.png](battlefield_lakeside_night__take-01.png) — **Selected**; usable night concept.
  The diagonal shoreline, submerged rocks, reeds, low eroded banks, forest edge and open grass retain the accepted source layout. Cool moonlight and blue water reflections replace the golden daytime treatment; shore access and underwater depth remain readable. No boats, buildings, figures or artificial lights were added.

Magenta is an extraction background, not transparency. These concept sources are not integrated into Godot.
